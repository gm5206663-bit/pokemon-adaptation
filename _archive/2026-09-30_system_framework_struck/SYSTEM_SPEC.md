# SYSTEM_SPEC.md — the power law, complete, day one

**Built from R14-A (no terminal past Salamence) and the Ultra Moon receipt.**
Grade ladder per F9: **Low / Mid / High / Top / Ultimate**.
Every grade has a **different name** (v5.2). 100% is the **trigger**, never the
end (F14, v5.1).

---

## 1. Two ladders, kept apart

The serial runs two independent ladders. Conflating them is the mistake that
broke earlier power systems.

**The body ladder** — species evolution. Canon gives three rungs. R14-A extends
it.

**The craft ladder** — techniques. Six lines, each running Low to Ultimate.

A body rung never grants a craft rung. A craft rung never grants a body rung.
They advance on separate clocks and the clocks are metered separately in
`METERS.md`.

## 3. The craft ladder — six lines

### Fusible family 1 — IMPACT

Three Low techniques, all impact, all body. Per Footwork Fusion v5.4 they do not
climb separately. **They fuse.**

> **Hard Head (Low)** — the headbutt itself.
> **Falling Practice (Low)** — the cliff dive that teaches the body to arrive.
> **Braced Stance (Low)** — taking the blow without yielding.

**Hard Head 100% + Falling Practice 100% + Braced Stance 100% fuse into:**

| Grade | Name | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Mid | **Cliff-Breaker** | one impact line, not three. The dive and the brace are inside the strike |
| High | **Boulder-Cleaver** | canon's own claim, earned: the head *"can cleave a boulder in one strike"* (Sword entry) |
| Top | **Sky-Sunder** | the strike carries the fall's momentum with no fall to start it |
| Ultimate | **Heaven-Cleaver** | unruled in detail. Named so the ladder has no ceiling |

**This is the direct port of the Footwork Fusion Law.** Which fool masters so
many headbutt techniques separately? They fuse.

## 4. The fusion law, restated for this serial

- Three suitable **Lows at 100%** fuse into one **Mid at 1%**.
- Three suitable **Mids at 100%** fuse into one **High at 1%**.
- **Suitable means same family.** Impact fuses with impact. Perception with
  perception. Never across families. No random fusion.
- **Fusion is not optional and not reversible.** Once three Lows reach 100% the
  fusion happens that turn. A technique does not sit at 100% waiting.
- **The fused line gets one name per grade**, and the constituent Lows stop
  being tracked separately.

## 5. What Adaptation Talent does to all of this

The talent does not grant rungs. It **prices** them. Full detail in
`ADAPTATION_TALENT_BAGON.md`. In short:

- It works on what the OC's real existence actually gives it. A Bagon that never
  hits stone never advances Hard Head, and the talent will not fake it.
- It is **embodied only**. No window, no menu, no screen, no voice.
- Every crossing answers the five questions **before** it is written.
- It never creates from nothing, needs a cause, and always leaves a limitation.

## 6. Walls

- **The Bagon cannot speak.** Ever. (RAILS k04)
- **No count-numbers in prose.** The ladder lives in this file and in
  `STATUS.md`, never in a sentence. (RAILS k02)
- **No terminal.** Nothing on either ladder ends. (F14, R14-A)
- **Nothing is granted.** Every rung has a cause, and the cause is a receipt.
  Where R14-A permits a past-canon rung, the cause is *the craft earned it*, and
  that has to be shown on page, not asserted.
