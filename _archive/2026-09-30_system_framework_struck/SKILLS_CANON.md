# SKILLS_CANON.md — technique descriptions in canon's own register

Written the way a Pokédex entry or an anime attack description reads, because
that is the register the serial borrows and the register the reader recognises.
Every entry separates **canon receipt** from **agent-proposed**, and the
separation is never blurred.

**Confidence tags:** `canon` (source named) · `reported` · `design` (agent-
proposed, no external receipt) · `user ruling`.

---

## The Impact line

### Hard Head (Low) — `canon` for the move, `design` for the meter

The headbutt. Canon's own words for what it does to the skull: *"Its steel-hard
head can shatter boulders"* (FireRed / LeafGreen), and *"Bagon bashes itself
against large rocks, until its head eventually grows as hard as steel"* — the
anime's own Pokédex entry, AG043.

**How it reads on page:** a single downward drive from the brow, not the crown.
The neck does most of the work and the body follows late. Against stone it makes
a sound like a dropped tool. Against something living it makes no sound at all.

**The ability that makes it sustainable — `canon`:** **Rock Head**. Bulbapedia
and pokemondb both describe it as preventing recoil damage from moves such as
Double-Edge. In this serial that is not a battle trivia fact, it is the reason
the meter can be pushed at all: a Bagon without Rock Head would break itself
long before Hard Head reached 100%.

### Cliff-Breaker (Mid) — `design`, produced by fusion

Not a canon move. It is what Hard Head, Falling Practice and Braced Stance
become when all three reach 100% and fuse.

**How it reads on page:** the dive is inside the strike. There is no run-up and
no jump; the body simply arrives with the weight of a fall it never took. The
ground under the landing cracks in a line, not a crater.

### Boulder-Cleaver (High) — `design`, but with a canon warrant

Canon claims the result plainly: a head *"hard enough to cleave a boulder in one
strike"* (Sword). This rung is where the serial finally shows that claim being
true rather than quoted.

### Sky-Sunder (Top) and Heaven-Cleaver (Ultimate) — `design`

Unruled in detail. Named so the ladder has no ceiling. Detail is written when
the serial is close enough to need it, not before.

## The Perception line

### Keen Nose (Low) — `design`

The world by scent, before the world by sight. A newborn Bagon's first map of
Kanto is smell: cold stone, wet leaf, the mineral tang of a route after rain.

### Watching (Low) — `design`

The tell, read before it fires. The meter only advances on an *early* read —
being right afterwards does not count, because being right afterwards is not a
skill.

### Stillness (Low) — `canon`-adjacent

Supported by the Sword entry: *"Bagon is a solitary Pokémon that doesn't form
groups with others of its kind."* Solitude is canonical for this species, which
makes patience a native trait rather than an invented one.

### Hunter's Eye (Mid), Battle Sense (High), War Mind (Top), Ten-Thousand-Fight Mind (Ultimate) — `design`

The fused line. Names only until the serial reaches them.

## The Dragon fire line

Three of these are canon moves and one is not, and the difference matters for
how checkable the serial is.

| Rung | Name | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Low | **Ember** | `canon` move — in Bagon's learnset across generations |
| Mid | **Dragon Breath** | `canon` move |
| High | **Dragon Pulse** | `canon` move |
| Top | **Dragon Ruin** | `design` |
| Ultimate | **Star-Fall Breath** | `design` |

**Ember, how it reads on page:** not a jet of fire. A cough of heat that barely
reaches, and the smell of singed grass before any of it is visible. For most of
the serial this is the OC's worst technique and it stays that way.

**Dragon Breath, how it reads on page:** a cone of pressure with light in it. The
first time it lands on something that cannot simply be burned, the difference
between fire and dragon becomes obvious to the OC and not to anyone watching.

## The Hide line

### Tucked Hide (Low) — `design`

Taking the blow with the hide instead of the bone. Learned by being hit, which
is the only way anything here is learned.

### Iron Cocoon (High) — `design`, placed to foreshadow

Placed at High because Shelgon is the cocoon rung and Shelgon does not move. The
craft line leans toward the body rung. **This is a design choice with no
external receipt and it is marked as one.** If it ever reads as convenient rather
than earned, it gets re-priced.

## The Flight line — LOCKED

**Nothing here exists before the body reaches Salamence.**

Canon's whole treatment of Bagon is the deferral. Ruby: *"Bagon has a dream of
one day soaring in the sky. In doomed efforts to fly, this Pokémon hurls itself
off cliffs."* The word canon uses is **doomed**. The serial honours that word for
two full body rungs.

| Rung | Name | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Low | **First Lift** | `design` |
| Mid | **Riding the Wind** | `design` |
| High | **Storm Crossing** | `design` |
| Top | **Sky Dominion** | `design` |
| Ultimate | **All-Sky** | `design` |

**First Lift, how it will read:** the serial's payoff, and R14-A softened it
knowingly. Salamence is not the end, so the flight is not a conclusion — it is
the first rung of a new ladder. The moment still lands. It just does not close
anything, and the author chose that.

## What is deliberately absent

- **Move power values, accuracy, PP, base stats.** All of it. Numbers in prose
  are banned (k02) and the franchise's numbers are the single biggest pull
  toward a status screen, which the locked Adaptation Talent core forbids at the
  definition level.
- **Type-chart arithmetic.** The OC *understands* types as a system (R4) but the
  serial never shows a chart. It shows a Bagon that has been burned learning
  what water does to fire.
- **Any named "ultimate technique."** Nothing is a finisher. The ladder has no
  terminal, so nothing gets to be the last move.
