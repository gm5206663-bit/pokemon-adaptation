technique chains and the fusion-at-100% rule are the **Ledger System** from the
Soul Land serials. They belong to an OC who holds a cheat System.

narrator guide adviser companion spirit familiar second mind not separate
consciousness not parasite symbiote possession blessing detachable."*

The agent wrote that clause into `ADAPTATION_TALENT_BAGON.md` and then built a
percentage meter table in the next file. That is a contradiction, and the author
caught it.
