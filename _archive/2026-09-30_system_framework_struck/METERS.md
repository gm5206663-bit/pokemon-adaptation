# METERS.md — every meter, with its own pace law

**Every technique advances by a counted input, at a pace this file sets.** No
meter advances by montage, by feeling, or because a chapter needs it to.
Counters live here and in `STATUS.md`, never in prose (RAILS k02).

The pacing model is the honest-yield law (F13) and the honest-pace law (F8):
progress is slow, uneven, and earned, and a wall held for a long time is correct
behaviour rather than a stalled serial.

---

## Active meters

### IMPACT family — all three fuse at 100%

| Meter | Unit of progress | Pace law | Cap at start |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hard Head** | one *solid* impact on stone. Glancing blows and impacts on soft ground do not count | 1% per 40 counted impacts. Slows by a tenth at every tenth of the way | 100% at roughly 4,000 impacts, before the slowdown |
| **Falling Practice** | one *survived* descent. A fall that ends in unconsciousness does not count as survived | 1% per 25 descents, but only from a height the body has already survived once | 100% at roughly 2,500 descents |
| **Braced Stance** | one blow taken without yielding ground | 1% per 60 blows. Slows sharply against anything with real weight behind it | 100% at roughly 6,000 blows |

**Fusion:** when all three reach 100% in the same chapter, **Cliff-Breaker Mid
starts at 1% that same turn.** The three meters close and stop being tracked.
This is not optional and not reversible (SYSTEM_SPEC §4).

### PERCEPTION family — all three fuse at 100%

| Meter | Unit of progress | Pace law |
|---|---|---|
| **Keen Nose** | one new scent correctly identified against later confirmation | 1% per 30 confirmations |
| **Watching** | one opponent's tell correctly read *before* the tell fires | 1% per 40 correct reads. A read confirmed afterwards does not count — it has to be early |
| **Stillness** | one hour held without moving, while something worth noticing happens | 1% per 90 hours |

### INDEPENDENT lines

| Meter | Unit of progress | Pace law |
|---|---|---|
| **Ember** | one successful ignition | 1% per 120 ignitions. Fails often early; failure still counts if the attempt was honest |
| **Tucked Hide** | one impact absorbed with the hide rather than the bone | 1% per 80 absorptions |
| **Flight line** | **LOCKED.** Opens at 1% on the day Salamence is reached | not metered until unlocked |

## The wall law (F12)

Meters do not run in a straight line. Three walls are set now, before chapter 1,
so that hitting one is recognisable as design rather than as a stall:

- **Wall 1 — the fusion wall.** All three Impact meters can reach 99% and sit
  there for chapters, because the third one lags. That is intentional. The
  fusion is a payoff and payoffs are held.
- **Wall 2 — the Shelgon wall.** Nothing in the craft ladder advances while the
  body is inside Shelgon. Canon's Shelgon is a cocoon: it does not move. A
  chapter set entirely inside Shelgon has no craft progress at all, and that is
  a chapter about waiting, which is the correct subject.
- **Wall 3 — the flight wall.** The Flight line stays locked through the whole
  of Bagon and Shelgon. Roughly two body rungs of a dream being deferred. This
  is the premise and it is not shortened for pacing.

## Honest yield (F13)

Progress that is slotted runs continuously, including while the OC sleeps. A
body that has learned to brace keeps bracing. Progress that is *not* slotted —
anything requiring intent, aim or a partner — only advances when the OC is awake
and trying.

This is what stops the serial from having to explain why the OC is strong. It is
strong because months passed and months are counted.

---

## What is deliberately NOT metered

- **Hunger, injury and recovery** are tracked in `STATUS.md`, not here. They are
  states, not skills.
- **Relationships** are tracked in `RELATIONSHIPS.md` with a knowledge firewall
  per character. Trust is not a percentage and this file refuses to make it one.
- **Body rung** is not metered. It advances on canon's own condition plus, past
  Salamence, on the craft ladder having earned it (R14-A). It is never a
  counter.
