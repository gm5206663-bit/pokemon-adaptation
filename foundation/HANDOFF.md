## The four things that break this serial

1. **A panel in prose.** Banned outright (k05, k06). A panel would be a lie in
   the OC's voice: it has no numbers and no narrator. `STATUS.md` counts, prose
   shows, and neither waits on the other.
2. **A count-number in prose.** k02. The franchise is numbers-shaped and pulls
   every writer toward levels and damage. Measure, do not argue.
3. **The OC knowing the plot.** R4 is rules-not-events. If a scene has it
   anticipating something, the scene is wrong and a firewall was breached.
4. **A terminal.** Nothing ends. F14, confirmed by R14-A. If you write a
   technique as mastered, you have broken the law and the gate should catch it.

## Boundaries

- **Do not weaken a gate to pass it.** Fix the work or fix the panel, never the
  check.
- **Record author rulings verbatim.** Never paraphrase one into something better.
- **Add-only housekeeping.** Superseded material is archived with a dated
  receipt, never silently deleted.
- **Sync every mirror the same turn** you change state: `STATUS.md`,
  `SERIAL_LOG.md`, `TIMELINE.md`, and the Control Centre registry.
- **Nothing crosses between fandoms.** This repo is its own wall.
- **Fan-work law:** non-commercial, reading copies only, the disclaimer in
  `NOTICE.md` travels with everything.
