## The move that the whole serial is built on

### Headbutt — `canon`

Canon's own words for what this species does with its skull:

- *"Bagon has a dream of one day soaring in the sky. In doomed efforts to fly,
  this Pokémon hurls itself off cliffs. As a result of its dives, its head has
  grown tough and as hard as tempered steel."* — Ruby
- *"Bagon bashes itself against large rocks, until its head eventually grows as
  hard as steel."* — the anime's own Pokédex entry, episode AG043
- *"Its steel-hard head can shatter boulders."* — FireRed / LeafGreen
- *"It also has a head hard enough to cleave a boulder in one strike."* — Sword

**How it reads on page:** a downward drive from the brow, not the crown. The
neck does the work and the body arrives late. Against stone it sounds like a
dropped tool. Against something alive it makes no sound at all.

**The ability that makes it sustainable — `canon`:** **Rock Head.** Bulbapedia
and pokemondb both describe it as preventing recoil damage from moves such as
Double-Edge. In this serial that is not battle trivia — it is the reason a Bagon
can spend a winter hitting rock without destroying itself.

### The cliff dive — `canon` behaviour, not a move

Canon treats this as what the species *does*, not as an attack. Ruby's word for
the attempts is **doomed**, and the serial honours that word.

**How it reads on page:** not a technique. A habit. The same ledge, the same
drop, over and over, and a body that gets better at arriving.

## Fire and dragon

| Move | Status | How it reads |
|---|---|---|
| **Ember** | `canon`, in Bagon's learnset | not a jet of fire. A cough of heat that barely reaches, and the smell of singed grass before any of it is visible. For most of the serial this is the worst thing the OC can do |
| **Dragon Breath** | `canon` | a cone of pressure with light in it. The first time it lands on something that cannot simply be burned, the difference between fire and dragon becomes obvious to the OC and to nobody watching |
| **Dragon Claw** | `canon` | available later in the learnset. Held until the body can carry it |
| **Dragon Rage** | `canon` | held. Listed so nobody reinvents it |

**No move past these is invented.** If the serial needs something a Bagon cannot
do, the honest answer is that it cannot do it.

## The rest of the learnset, for reference

Rage, Leer, Bite, Focus Energy, Protect, Slash, Scary Face, Crunch,
Double-Edge — all `canon`, all in Bagon's learnset across generations. Listed so
the serial never invents a move when a real one already fits.

**Double-Edge deserves a note:** it is a recoil move, and Rock Head cancels the
recoil. That combination is canon, it is specific to this species, and it is the
cleanest example in the whole kit of a canon fact doing story work. A Bagon can
throw its entire body at something and pay nothing for it. That is not a System
feature. It is a Pokédex entry.

## What is deliberately absent

- **Move power values, accuracy, PP, base stats.** All of it. Numbers in prose
  are banned (k02), and the franchise's numbers are the strongest pull toward a
  status screen — which the locked core forbids at the definition level.
- **Technique grades.** No Low, Mid, High, Top or Ultimate. Struck 2026-09-30.
- **Renamed evolution of a move.** Headbutt does not become "Cliff-Breaker". It
  stays Headbutt and the body gets better at it.
- **Fusion.** Moves do not merge. That was a System rule.
- **Any named finisher.** Nothing is a last move, because nothing ends.

## The flight, held

**Canon's whole treatment of Bagon is deferral.** Ruby: *"In doomed efforts to
fly, this Pokémon hurls itself off cliffs."*

Salamence is Dragon / Flying. The dream resolves there, in canon, on its own
terms — and this serial needs to add nothing to make that land.

What R14-A adds is only that **flying is not the end of the story.** Not a new
stage, not a new name, not a new move. Just: after the wings, there is still
more creature to become, and we will see it when we see it.
