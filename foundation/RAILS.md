# RAILS.md — this serial's own laws of record

These are the laws specific to this serial. The F-series laws of record (see
`how-to-write-fanfiction/VERSION_OF_RECORD.md` — 31 entries, F0-F26 + P12 + 7
structural) apply on top of them. Where a rail and an F-law conflict, the
F-law wins and this file gets a dated correction.

---

## Prose law

**k01 — clean and clear.** Band 2400-3400 words. Sentence average 14-18. No
sentence over 60. `over60 0`. `the-way` 0 per chapter. `bare` 0.

**k02 — no count-numbers in prose.** No digits in the body of a chapter. Levels,
HP, stats, damage, ages and dates all live in `STATUS.md`. This is the hardest
law in this serial because the franchise is numbers-shaped, and it is the one
that will be broken first if it is not measured.

**k03 — third person limited, the Bagon's side only.** The camera never leaves
the OC. Canon events are witnessed from outside, never narrated from inside a
canon character's head.

**k04 — the OC cannot speak.** No dialogue from the OC, ever. Its meaning is
carried by gesture, posture, sound and what other characters decide it means. A
chapter that needs the OC to explain something is a chapter with a structural
problem.

## Panel law

**k05 — panels default to NONE in prose.** Per the Devouring Dragon law: in a
non-human POV the story is the beast's. Full detail lives in
`foundation/STATUS.md`. Where a gain is genuinely new and on page, the prose
*shows* it and the panel in `STATUS.md` records it the same turn (F22).

**k06 — zero panel lines in a chapter body.** If a chapter needs a status dump,
the dump was earned in an earlier chapter and is being repeated.

## Power law

**k07 — no terminal.** Nothing is ever finished. Salamence is a floor, not a
ceiling (R14-A). What it continues into is unruled and stays unruled until the
serial reaches it.

**k08 — no System.** No meters, no percentages, no grades, no renamed technique
chains, no fusion. Those are the Soul Land Ledger machinery and they belong to an
OC holding a cheat System. This OC holds Adaptation Talent. Struck 2026-09-30 on
the author's word; receipt in `_archive/2026-09-30_system_framework_struck/`.

**k09 — every stage crossing answers the five questions before it is written.**
What changed? What caused it? What did he already possess that made this
possible? What failed first? What limitation remains?

**k10 — Adaptation Talent is embodied only.** No window, menu, screen, voice,
narrator, guide, companion or second mind. The locked core forbids it at the
definition level.

## Canon law

**k11 — the anime is load-bearing for beats; everything else is an anchor.**
Games, Adventures, spin-offs and Bulbapedia are admissible for a *fact*, never
load-bearing for a *beat* (R1 as reconciled).

**k12 — the Bagon's presence in Kanto is a logged divergence.** It has a cause
(R13-A) and the cause is never spoken on page in Season 1.

**k13 — Hoenn is unnamed.** No eastern land is named until the author rules it.

**k14 — the OC knows rules, not events.** It understands types and evolution as
a system. It has never heard of Ash Ketchum, Team Rocket, or anything that has
not happened yet.

**k15 — nothing enters a chapter unverified.** Every canon claim needs a receipt
in `CANON_GROUND.md` with a source and a confidence tag. Wiki knowledge is ore,
not law.

**k16 — canon beats are not displaced.** Ash still gets Pikachu. Misty still
loses a bike. The Ho-Oh still crosses the rainbow. The Bagon is added, never
centered.

## Ship law

**k17 — no chapter ships without its gate passing.** The gate is built before
chapter 1, not after. A gate that has never caught anything is decoration.

**k18 — sync every mirror the same turn.** `STATUS.md`, `SERIAL_LOG.md`,
`TIMELINE.md`, the Control Centre registry. "Later" is how state rots.

**k19 — the author's rulings are recorded verbatim.** Never paraphrased into
something better.
