# PLACES.md — the map, and what is deliberately blank

## Kanto — receipted

The region. Canon's Season 1 setting (R2). `canon`, IL001.

**Named places that appear in IL001, all receipted in `CANON_GROUND.md` §1:**

- **Pallet Town** — where Ash lives and where Oak's laboratory is. The OC is not
  there.
- **Viridian City** — where the Pokémon Center is that Misty sends Ash toward.
- The route between them, the waterfall, and the sky a Ho-Oh crosses.

**Everything else in Kanto is blank until its ore file exists.** This is
deliberate. Naming a town the serial has not receipted is how a canon error gets
into chapter 12 and has to be unwound from six chapters of continuity.

## Where the OC is

**Unruled, and intentionally.** The foundation does not place the Bagon on the
map, because *where it wakes up* is a chapter 1 decision that belongs with the
chapter 1 plan, not with the foundation. What is fixed:

- It is in **Kanto**.
- It is **alone**. Canon says the species is solitary and does not form groups
  (Sword entry, `canon`).
- It has **stone** and it has **height**, because canon says this species hits
  rock and throws itself off cliffs. Both are receipted in `CANON_GROUND.md` §3.

That is enough to write chapter 1 and no more is needed yet.

## Hoenn

**Unnamed on page. (k13, R13-A)** The eastern land the Bagon came from is never
named in Season 1. It may never be named. Hoenn does not exist as a place anyone
in Kanto can point to, and naming it would spend a reveal the anime holds for
another five years.

## Economy.md, folded in here

The method calls for an `ECONOMY.md`. For a wild Pokémon with no trainer, money
is meaningless, so the economy is a **resource ledger** instead:

| Resource | Scarcity | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Food** | the real pressure | a body that trains needs intake. Hunger is tracked in `STATUS.md` as a state |
| **Stone that does not break** | scarce | the head has to meet rock that outlasts it. Finding a boulder worth a winter is a chapter event |
| **Height** | seasonal | the cliff has to be one the body has already survived. Canon's word for the attempts is *doomed*, and the serial honours that |
| **Shelter** | contested | a solitary animal still needs somewhere to be unconscious safely |
| **Territory** | none, yet | Domain **Interface** of the locked core is empty. The first territory will be the first thing that domain has to work on |

**No money, no Pokéballs, no items** — until a human enters the story. The OC
cannot use a Pokéball and has never seen one.
