deliberately empty of content, because writing it before the rulings exist is
exactly how a serial ends up rebuilding chapter 30.

the author's other serials, the locks, and the world rules.

## Inherited, already decided

- **Clean-and-clear prose law** — band 2400-3400 words, sentence average 14-18,
  no sentence over 60, `over60 0`, `the-way 0`, `bare 0`.
- **Gate before ship** — no chapter ships without its gate passing. A gate that
  has never caught anything is decoration.
- **Panels per F22** — full detail in `STATUS.md`, prose shows only what
  changed on page.
- **Canon receipts** — nothing enters a chapter unverified. Wiki knowledge is
  ore, not law.
- **Author rulings outrank agent design**, recorded verbatim, never paraphrased.
