## The premise in one paragraph

A Bagon — a Hoenn species with no business existing in Kanto in 1997, brought
west years ago by someone who left no record — wakes into the world on the day a
ten-year-old boy oversleeps and is handed an electric mouse that does not want
him. The Bagon holds Adaptation Talent, the author's locked universal core: an
innate, non-sentient, embodied principle that continuously works on whatever the
holder's real existence actually gives it. It understands types and evolution as
a system and has never heard of the plot. It cannot speak. Every relationship it
will ever have runs through gesture, and every human in Kanto who meets it meets
something their Pokédex cannot name.

## Why canon needs this OC to exist

**It does not.** That is the correct answer and it is the law. The Bagon is
added, never centered. Ash still gets Pikachu, still meets Misty, still sees a
Ho-Oh. The Bagon is in the same region on the same clock and its life touches
canon only where the tracks make contact, and every contact is logged.

## The three inherited laws that shape everything

**1. Adaptation Talent is embodied only.** The locked core forbids a system
window, a menu, a status screen, a voice, a narrator, a guide, a companion, a
second mind. In a franchise shaped like a game, this is the load-bearing wall.
Levels, HP, stats and damage live in `STATUS.md` and never in a sentence.

**2. Nothing is ever finished (R14-A).** Bagon to Shelgon to Salamence is the
canon body, and Salamence is a floor, not a ceiling. What comes after is unruled
and stays unruled until the serial reaches it.

**3. There is no System here.** No meters, no percentages, no grades, no renamed
technique chains, no fusion. That machinery belongs to the Soul Land serials
where the OC holds a cheat System. This OC holds Adaptation Talent, and growth is
shown through the body over time rather than counted. Struck 2026-09-30 on the
author's word.

## The receipts that made this buildable

- **Ultra Moon:** *"Some theories suggest that its behavior of forcefully bashing
  its head into things stimulates cells that affect its evolution."* Canon has
  already ruled the headbutting is a **cause** of change. The cliff-diving is
  therefore training. See `CANON_GROUND.md` §3.
- **Bulbapedia:** Bagon is *"unavailable prior to Generation III."* This is what
  makes R13 a logged divergence rather than a mistake.
- **IL001 on page:** the Pokédex is a physical object Ash uses. It has no data on
  a Bagon. That is a receipt-backed beat, not an invention.

## Locks (what is fixed)

- The OC is a **Bagon**, male, and cannot speak.
- The clock starts on **IL001**, Kanto, 1997.
- **Hoenn is unnamed** on page until the author opens it.
- The OC knows **rules, not events**.
- Adaptation Talent is **embodied only**.
- Canon beats are **not displaced**.

## Bans (what is forbidden)

- No system window, menu, status screen, voice or narrator, ever.
- No count-numbers in prose. No digits in the body of a chapter.
- No canon character learning what the OC knows.
- No naming Hoenn, or any eastern land, before the author rules it.
- No technique reaching a terminal.
- No chapter shipping without its gate passing.

## Lessons inherited from the other serials

- **From Qing Ling:** the OC stands *beside* the host, not in the host's place.
  Two tracks, one clock, one set of streets.
- **From Grey Wolf:** clean and clear. Band 2400-3400, sentence average 14-18,
  no sentence over 60, `the-way` 0, `bare` 0.
- **From Qian Xun Ji:** every stage crossing answers the five questions before
  it is written.
- **From Devouring Dragon:** panels default to none in a non-human POV. The
  story is the beast's.
- **From the whole workspace:** a fact maintained in two places will be wrong in
  one of them, and the check reads the other.
