## R3 in full — the one worth arguing about

**Recommendation: the OC is a Pokémon, and the human trainer is the canon lens.**

Your strongest recent serial is **Qing Ling**, where the OC is not the host —
it is the *Golden-Veined Blue Silver Grass beside Tang Wulin*, running a
two-track growth law (R6: Track A is the spirit's own life and its crossings;
Track B is the host interface). That serial solves the hardest problem in
gated fanfiction: how an OC can be present in every canon scene without
stealing the canon protagonist's causality. The OC is *alongside*, not *instead
of*.

Pokémon hands you that structure for free. A Pokémon-POV serial gets:

- **Interiority the canon never gives you.** The games' protagonist is a silent
  player-insert and the anime's Pokémon speak in their own names. Nobody has
  written the inside of this. There is no canon interiority to contradict.
- **Every canon scene available without displacement.** The trainer still does
  what the trainer does. The OC is in the room, not in the chair.
- **A growth law that is native to the franchise** instead of imported. Evolution
  *is* your Evolution Chain Law. A Pokémon literally changes body and name at a
  threshold, and there is no such thing as a terminal — exactly F14.
- **A built-in knowledge firewall.** A Pokémon does not know what its trainer
  knows. That is a free WHO-KNOWS-WHAT matrix, which is the file your method
  says is the whole defence against the next agent letting a character find out
  early because the plot needs it.

**The cost, stated honestly:** a Pokémon POV cannot speak. Every relationship
runs through gesture, and dialogue is carried by the humans around it. That is
a real constraint and it is the main reason to pick trainer-POV instead.

**Alternatives:**
- **B — Trainer POV.** More conventional, far easier dialogue, but you are then
  writing beside Ash/Red/a custom trainer, and the displacement problem comes
  back in full.
- **C — Dual POV, alternating.** Qing Ling's two tracks, made explicit. Most
  flexible, hardest to hold — the two tracks must share one clock and one set
  of streets, which is your Dual-Track law and it is expensive.

## R4 in full — why the recommendation is *no* meta knowledge

Four of your serials give the OC knowledge from outside the world: Grey Wolf
(*"OC grown Earth full meta"*, F0), Qian Xun Ji (reincarnator with SL1+SL2+SL3
plus wiki and fandom), Stark Heir and Eternal Varun both carrying Adaptation
Talent. It works, and it is clearly a taste. The reason to rule it **off** here
is narrow and practical:

**Pokémon meta knowledge is mostly a walkthrough.** Knowing the canon gives a
Pokémon OC the gym order, the wild encounters, the legendary locations and the
type matchups — which collapses into route optimisation, and route
optimisation is the least interesting thing that can happen in this world. In
Soul Land the meta gives you *cultivation law*, which is a way of thinking. In
Pokémon it gives you *a map*, which is a cheat sheet.

If you want the meta anyway, the version that survives is: **the OC knows the
world's rules but not its events** — it understands types and evolution as a
system, and has never heard of the plot. That keeps the firewall meaningful and
keeps canon's surprises intact.

**Alternatives:** full Earth-meta reincarnator (your existing pattern) · no
meta at all · rules-only meta as described above.
