# CHARACTERS.md — cast, canon and ours

## Ours

### The Bagon — `<readout>` (R11 open)

**Canon base, all receipted in `CANON_GROUND.md` §2:** #0371, Dragon-type,
introduced Generation III, species name "Rock Head Pokémon", 0.6 m, 42.1 kg,
Slow growth rate, Dragon egg group, ability **Rock Head** with hidden **Sheer
Force**.

**Canon character, which is the whole premise:** it dreams of flying and cannot.
Ruby — *"Bagon has a dream of one day soaring in the sky. In doomed efforts to
fly, this Pokémon hurls itself off cliffs."* Sword — *"a solitary Pokémon that
doesn't form groups with others of its kind."*

**What the serial adds:** Adaptation Talent, the locked universal core. And the
fact of being here — a Hoenn species in Kanto, years before anyone in Kanto
could have heard of Hoenn.

**What it cannot do:** speak. Ever. (k04)

**What it does not know:** the plot, its own species name, that it holds a
talent, where it came from. See `RELATIONSHIPS.md`.

**Personality, provisional and author-strikable:** stubborn in the specific way
canon describes — it will hit the same rock for a month. Not clever in a human
way. Patient because it is solitary, not because it is wise. Bad-tempered about
cliffs and tender about nothing else, yet.

## Canon — present in Kanto from IL001

All states are `UNKNOWN` toward the OC. See `RELATIONSHIPS.md` for the full
firewall table and the `earliest_change` field on every row.

- **Ash Ketchum** — ten years old, Pallet Town, oversleeps on the day he is due
  to receive his first Pokémon. Gets Pikachu. `canon`, IL001.
- **Pikachu** — the starter Oak has left, "with a problem". Aloof, distrusts
  Ash, shocks him on pickup. Drives off the Spearow flock after Ash shields it.
  `canon`, IL001.
- **Professor Oak** — gives Ash the Pokédex and six Poké Balls. `canon`, IL001.
  The character most likely to eventually classify the OC, and therefore a
  reveal worth holding.
- **Misty** — fishes Ash and Pikachu out of the waterfall, directs them to
  Viridian, loses a bike. `canon`, IL001.
- **Gary Oak** — starts his journey the same day with a Squirtle. `canon`, IL001.
- **The Spearow flock** — the episode's actual threat. `canon`, IL001.
- **The Ho-Oh** — crosses the rainbow at the episode's close. `canon`, IL001.
  The serial will not touch it. It is canon's, and it crosses canon's sky
  whether or not the OC is watching.

## Not cast — and why

- **Team Rocket.** Exist in the continuity but are not positioned. If they see
  the OC they will want to sell it, and that is a thread the serial can open
  whenever it wants and should not open early.
- **Whoever brought the Bagon west.** R13-A leaves this unruled. It may never be
  cast at all.
- **Michelle and Sawyer.** Both own Bagons in the anime, but in the Hoenn era
  (AG043 and the XY series). They are years away and are noted only so nobody
  reinvents them.

## The displacement rule

**k16 — canon beats are not displaced.** Ash still gets Pikachu. Misty still
loses a bike. The Ho-Oh still crosses the rainbow. If a chapter has the OC doing
something a canon character was going to do, the chapter is wrong, not the
canon.
