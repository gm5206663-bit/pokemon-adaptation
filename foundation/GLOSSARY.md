# GLOSSARY.md — terms, and words this serial will not use

## Canon terms, receipted

- **Bagon** — #0371, Dragon-type, Generation III. The OC's species. `canon`
- **Shelgon** — #0372, the cocoon stage, level 30. `canon`
- **Salamence** — #0373, Dragon/Flying, level 50. A floor, not a ceiling (R14-A). `canon`
- **Rock Head** — ability. Prevents recoil damage from moves such as
  Double-Edge. The reason a Bagon can spend a winter hitting rock without
  destroying itself. `canon`
- **Sheer Force** — Bagon's hidden ability. `canon`. Not used in this serial
  unless a ruling opens it, because two abilities on one OC is a power decision
  the author should make deliberately rather than inherit.
- **Pokédex** — the device Oak gives Ash in IL001. Has no entry for a Bagon. `canon`
- **Indigo League** — the anime's Season 1, Kanto. The spine (R2). `canon`

## Our terms

- **Adaptation Talent** — the locked universal core. Never named on page. Never
  displayed. See `ADAPTATION_TALENT_BAGON.md`.
- **No System** — the law that there are no meters, percentages, grades, renamed
  technique chains or fusion. Struck 2026-09-30. See `POWER_LAW.md`.
- **Firewall** — the knowledge state of a character toward the OC, with an
  `earliest_change` field. Seven states, closed list.
- **Ore** — distilled canon facts in `canon_extract/`. Ore is not law. Nothing
  enters a chapter unverified.
- **Receipt** — a source-backed claim in `CANON_GROUND.md` with a confidence tag.
- **The body's stages** — Bagon, Shelgon, Salamence, and then unruled (R14-A).

## Retired house-words — do not use

Each serial carries its own banned list. This one starts with the workspace's
standing bans and adds two of its own.

| Word or phrase | Why |
|---|---|
| `the way X` | capped at zero per chapter (k01). `the-way 0` |
| `bare` | `bare 0` |
| any count-number | k02. No digits in a chapter body |
| `system`, `interface`, `menu`, `screen`, `level up`, `status` | the locked core forbids the concept. These words cannot appear in prose even as metaphor, because metaphor is how a status screen sneaks back in |
| `he knew that one day...` | prophecy voice. The OC knows rules, not events (k14) |
| `destiny`, `fated`, `chosen` | the OC is added, never centered (k16). It was not chosen by anything |

## The plain-language law

Plain words first. Sentence average 14-18. Nothing over 60. The register is a
creature's register: what it smells, what hurts, what is in the way. It is not a
register that supports abstraction, and the prose should not pretend otherwise.
