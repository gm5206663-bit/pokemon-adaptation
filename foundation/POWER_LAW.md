# POWER_LAW.md — how this OC gets stronger

**This is not a System.** There are no meters, no percentages, no grades, no
renamed technique chains and no fusion rules. Those belong to the Soul Land
serials where the OC holds a cheat System. This OC holds **Adaptation Talent**,
and the locked core forbids the System at the definition level.

Struck 2026-09-30 on the author's word. Receipt in
`_archive/2026-09-30_system_framework_struck/RECEIPT.md`.

---

## 1. The body

Canon gives three stages and the serial takes them as they are.

| Stage | Type | Canon condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Bagon** | Dragon | the starting body. #0371, Generation III |
| **Shelgon** | Dragon | level 30 in the games. In this serial, the body's own condition, written as a body's condition and never as a number |
| **Salamence** | Dragon / Flying | level 50 in the games. Same rule |

**Past Salamence: unruled, and staying that way.**

R14-A ruled that the chain **continues** — Salamence is a floor, not a ceiling.
It did not rule what it continues into. Three names were invented here and struck
the same day:

> Author: "We later see this thing."

So the ladder above Salamence is **open**. It gets a shape when the serial
reaches it and not before. What is fixed is only the principle: it does not end.

**Why the chain continuing is not a System feature.** In the Soul Land serials,
"no terminal" is a rule about technique grades. Here it is a rule about a *life*.
A creature that dreamed of flying for two whole stages, and flew, is not
finished because it flew. That needs no machinery to be true.

## 2. The moves

**Canon moves, learned the way Pokémon learn them.** No invented technique
lines, no renaming at thresholds. Bagon's own learnset across generations
includes, among others: Rage, Leer, Bite, Headbutt, Focus Energy, Ember, Dragon
Breath, Protect, Slash, Scary Face, Crunch, Dragon Claw, Double-Edge.

Receipts for the moves that matter are in `SKILLS_CANON.md`. Where a move is
canon it is marked `canon` with its source; where the serial has added something
it is marked `design` and it stays rare.

**What Adaptation Talent changes about moves:** not which ones exist, and not
what they are called. It changes **how well the body converts practice into
capability**. A Bagon that headbutts stone for a month is not the same Bagon
afterwards, and the talent is the reason the change is real rather than random.

## 3. The talent

Full detail in `ADAPTATION_TALENT_BAGON.md`. The shape of it:

- It is **innate, non-sentient and embodied**. It has no voice, no window, no
  opinion. It is not a companion and it does not advise.
- It works on **what the OC's real existence actually gives it** — experience,
  injury, recovery, repetition, whatever is genuinely accessible.
- It **does not create from nothing** and it **does not skip**.
- It always **leaves a limitation**.

**The five questions, at every crossing.** This is the only structure the serial
borrows, and it is borrowed because it is a *writing* discipline, not a game
mechanic:

> What changed? What caused it? What did he already possess that made this
> possible? What failed first? What limitation remains?

Answered **before** a crossing is written. A crossing that cannot answer "what
failed first?" is a crossing that was not earned.

## 4. How growth reads on page

This is the whole method, and it replaces the meters entirely.

| Never | Always |
|---|---|
| a percentage | the neck stops aching before the stone does |
| a level | it hit the same rock all winter and the rock lost |
| a technique name changing | the blow landed differently and the OC noticed |
| a grade being reached | something it could not do in autumn, it did in spring |
| a counter ticking | a season passing, shown by what the body can now carry |

**The rule:** growth is **shown through the body over time**, never counted. If
a reader could put a number on it, the sentence is wrong.

This is the same law that bans panels from prose (RAILS k05, k06), and it now
has a reason underneath it: there is nothing to panel. The OC has no numbers
because the serial has no numbers.

## 5. The one canon receipt doing the real work

**Pokémon Ultra Moon:** *"Some theories suggest that its behavior of forcefully
bashing its head into things stimulates cells that affect its evolution."*
(`canon`, via Bulbapedia — see `CANON_GROUND.md` §3.)

Canon has already ruled that the headbutting is a **cause** of change in this
species. That is the whole warrant for a serial about a Bagon that trains. It is
a real warrant, it is narrow, and it does not require a System to use.

## 6. What is forbidden

- No window, menu, screen, gauge, percentage, level or stat — in prose or in any
  foundation file. **This file is now subject to that rule too.**
- No technique renamed at a threshold.
- No fusion rule.
- No invented past-Salamence stage until the serial earns one.
- No terminal. Nothing is ever finished.
- No grant. Every change has a cause, and the cause is on page.
