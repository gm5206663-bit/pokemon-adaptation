Nothing is written here yet. Every slot is `pending` in

### Open ordering problem — must be resolved by receipt before IL002 is used

The episode order for the Indigo League is **not unambiguous**, and this is the
same trap the Miraculous project documented when it chose production order and
wrote broadcast order off as "anchors only."

Evidence of the conflict, found while building this file: one listing gives
IL002 as *"Pokémon Emergency!"*, while another gives the second episode as
*"Bulbasaur and the Hidden Village"*. Both cannot be the spine.

**Ruling needed, and not taken by the agent:** which ordering is load-bearing?
Until it is receipted, the serial stays inside IL001 and nothing downstream is
planned. Guessing here is exactly how a canon error gets baked into an arc
structure that is expensive to move later.

## Our road — the OC's clock, independent of canon's

The OC's clock runs on **days**, not episodes. This is deliberate: the anime's
pacing is not the Bagon's pacing, and forcing them to match would either freeze
the OC or rush it.

| Phase | Subject | Rough span |
|---|---|---|
| **Before the story** | the eastern land, unnamed, and the crossing west | unruled. R13-A says it happened years ago and no record was left |
| **Day 0** | alone in Kanto. Nobody knows it exists | chapter 1 |
| **The first long stretch** | stone, cliffs, being hit. Canon's word for the attempts is *doomed* | long, on purpose. Arc 1 |
| **Being seen** | the first human. Which one is author-gated | Arc 2 |
| **Shelgon** | the body stops moving | a whole arc of waiting. Arc 3 |
| **Salamence** | wings. Canon's own resolution | the payoff R14-A softened but did not remove. Arc 4 |
| **After** | unruled | the author: "We later see this thing" |

**No date in this table is fixed.** They are shaped by what happens to the body,
not by a schedule. When a chapter changes something true about the OC, this file
moves the same turn (k18).
