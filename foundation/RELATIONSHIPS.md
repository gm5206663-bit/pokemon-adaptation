# RELATIONSHIPS.md — cast web, and the knowledge firewalls

**The firewall is the whole defence.** Per the Control Centre protocol:
*"Author knowledge is not character knowledge. Every firewall needs
`earliest_change`. Without it, the next agent will helpfully let the character
find out early because the plot needs it. That field is the whole defence."*

**States, from the Control Centre's closed list:** `KNOWN` · `KNOWN PARTLY` ·
`SUSPICION` · `DISBELIEF` · `UNKNOWN` · `FALSE BELIEF` · `HIDDEN`. Adding a new
state is a law change and needs the author.

---

## The OC knows

| Topic | State | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Types and evolution as a system | `KNOWN` | R4, rules-not-events. Learned by living in the world, not downloaded |
| Its own body, accurately | `KNOWN` | Domain **Direct** of the locked core. Embodied, never narrated |
| The plot | `UNKNOWN` | **Hard firewall.** It has never heard of Ash Ketchum, Team Rocket, or anything that has not happened yet |
| What it is | `UNKNOWN` | Nobody has told it. There is no word for a Bagon in Kanto |
| That it holds a talent | `UNKNOWN` | The talent is never named, even to itself (ADAPTATION_TALENT_BAGON locks) |
| Hoenn, or any eastern land | `HIDDEN` | R13-A. It may not even have a concept of where it came from |
| Speech | `UNKNOWN` | It cannot speak and has never been able to. This is not a limitation it is working around; it is the shape of its life |

**If a scene ever has the OC anticipating a canon event, the scene is wrong.**
That is the test, and it is applied before drafting, not after.

## The relationships the serial actually runs

The OC has **no trainer, no bond, no territory**. Domain **Interface** of the
locked Adaptation Talent core — bonded weapons, artifacts, symbiotes, spirits,
bonds, technology, organizations, territory — is **empty at the start**.

That emptiness is the story engine, not a gap. The first bond this Bagon forms
will be the first thing Interface has ever had to work on, and it will change
what the talent can do. **Which bond comes first is author-gated and is not
proposed here**, because it is the serial's most consequential decision after the
premise and it should not be defaulted.

## Trust is not a percentage

This file refuses to give a relationship a number. A bond is either formed on
page or it is not. There is no meter for it, because there are no meters.
